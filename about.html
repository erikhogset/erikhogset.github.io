About Me

I’m Erik Hogset, a Mechanical Engineering student at Boston University. I grew up in Everett, Pennsylvania — a small town where building and fixing things was simply part of everyday life — and that curiosity eventually led me toward engineering.

I currently work as a Laboratory Assistant at BU’s Engineering Product Innovation Center (EPIC), where I support CNC machining, additive manufacturing, and hands-on prototyping for student and faculty projects. The role has given me extensive experience with fabrication tools, design iteration, and helping others bring engineering ideas into physical prototypes. Read more →

Earlier in college I worked at Starbucks, where I developed strong teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills in a fast-paced environment. That experience helped shape how I approach collaborative engineering work and customer-focused design.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy museums, traveling, hiking, and exploring places with strong cultural or historical character — whether that’s a quiet trail, a city skyline, or a museum courtyard.
