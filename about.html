About Me

I’m Erik Hogset, a Mechanical Engineering student at Boston University. I grew up in Everett, Pennsylvania — a small town where I spent a lot of time taking things apart, fixing them, and figuring out how they worked. This curiosity eventually led me toward engineering.

I currently work as a Laboratory Assistant at BU’s Engineering Product Innovation Center (EPIC), where I support CNC machining, additive manufacturing, and hands-on prototyping for student and faculty projects. The role has given me extensive experience with fabrication tools, design iteration, and helping others bring engineering ideas into physical prototypes. Read more →

Throughout most of my time at Boston University I worked at Starbucks alongside my coursework. The fast paced environment strenghtened my communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills, and taught me how to stay organized and focused under pressure. That experience continues to shape how I approach collaborative engineering work and user-focused design.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy museums, traveling, hiking, and exploring interesting and new places — whether that’s a quiet hiking trail, a city skyline, or a museum courtyard.
