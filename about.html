About Me

I’m Erik Hogset, a Mechanical Engineering student at Boston University. I grew up in Everett, Pennsylvania — a small town where making things and fixing things was just part of everyday life — and that curiosity eventually pulled me toward engineering.

I currently work as a Laboratory Assistant at BU’s Engineering Product Innovation Center (EPIC), where I help support CNC machining, additive manufacturing, and hands-on prototyping for student and faculty projects. I love being in spaces where ideas turn into physical things. Read more →

Outside of engineering, I spend a lot of time in museums, traveling, hiking, and being in nature. I’m especially drawn to art, design, and places with strong cultural or historical character — whether that’s a quiet trail, a city skyline, or a museum courtyard.
