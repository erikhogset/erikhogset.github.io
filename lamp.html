Smart Desk Lamp

A sensor-driven desk lamp prototype designed to improve accessibility, adjust brightness automatically, and reduce blue-light exposure over the course of the day.

Product Design Sensors Microcontrollers Prototyping

Prototype Demonstration

Demonstration of the final smart lamp prototype. A hand wave over the ultrasonic sensor cycles the lamp through brightness modes, while the overall light output and blue-light content are adjusted using sensor input and time-based control logic.

Project Overview

This project focused on creating a desk lamp that was both more accessible and more responsive to its environment than a standard lamp. The final system combined a motion-based user interface, automatic brightness control, time-dependent color adjustment, and a mechanically adjustable lamp head.

Rather than relying on small switches or manual brightness settings alone, the lamp was designed so a user could wave a hand over the motion sensor to change operating mode. This made the product easier to use for people with limited dexterity while also introducing adaptive light behavior.

What the Lamp Does

User Interaction

  • Hand motion over the ultrasonic sensor changes lamp mode
  • Modes cycle through dim, normal, bright, automatic, and off
  • Designed to be usable with limited dexterity
  • Lamp head can be repositioned with light force

Adaptive Lighting

  • Ambient light sensor adjusts brightness automatically in auto mode
  • Real-time clock changes blue-light percentage based on time of day
  • Brighter, cooler light during the day
  • Reduced blue-light output during evening and night

Sensing & Control Logic

Sensors

  • Ultrasonic sensor for motion-based input
  • Ambient light sensor for room brightness detection
  • Real-time clock for time-of-day lighting changes

Control Behavior

  • Wave input advances the lamp through brightness states
  • Automatic mode maps room lux to dim, normal, or bright output
  • RGB LED output is adjusted to reduce blue-light exposure at night
  • Microcontroller coordinates all sensing and output behavior

Mechanical & Product Design

The lamp was designed as a functional desktop product rather than only an electronics demo. The form integrated a stable base, vertical support structure, adjustable head, and internal routing for wires and components. The head adjustment mechanism was developed to hold position while still requiring only a small amount of force to move.

Technical Summary

Hardware

  • Ultrasonic motion sensor
  • Ambient light sensor
  • Real-time clock
  • Microcontroller-based control system
  • Adjustable mechanical lamp head

Performance Goals

  • Illuminate a desk-sized workspace
  • Support multiple brightness settings
  • Reduce blue-light output at night
  • Improve ease of use for people with limited mobility
  • Allow manual adjustment of lamp orientation

Key Takeaways

User-Centered Design

This project pushed me to think about accessibility and product interaction more intentionally by designing around limited dexterity, lighting comfort, and ease of adjustment.

Integrated Prototyping

I gained experience combining physical product design with sensing and control logic, while iterating on both the mechanical form and the behavior of the final system.
