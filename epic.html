Overview

The Engineering Product Innovation Center (EPIC) at Boston University is a student-focused prototyping and fabrication space designed to support hands-on engineering design and product development. As a lab assistant, I help students translate ideas into physical prototypes by providing guidance on fabrication tools, manufacturing methods, and safe machine operation.

My role involves assisting with a wide range of student projects across multiple engineering disciplines while maintaining the lab environment and ensuring equipment is used safely and effectively.

Responsibilities

  • Assist students with prototyping and fabrication across engineering design projects
  • Provide guidance on safe operation of fabrication equipment
  • Troubleshoot manufacturing issues during machining, printing, and assembly
  • Support rapid iteration of prototypes through multiple fabrication methods
  • Maintain lab equipment and ensure safe working conditions

Equipment & Tools

Machining

  • Manual 3-axis mill
  • CNC milling machines
  • Manual lathe
  • CNC lathe

Fabrication

  • Waterjet cutting
  • CO₂ laser cutting and engraving
  • FDM 3D printing
  • SLA resin 3D printing
