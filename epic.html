Overview

Boston University’s Engineering Product Innovation Center (EPIC) is a hands-on design and fabrication space that supports student project development across a wide range of prototyping and manufacturing processes. The lab includes manual and CNC machining, laser cutting, waterjet cutting, 3D printing, welding, metrology, and other fabrication resources that support iterative product development. [oai_citation:1‡Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/epic/equipment/)

As a lab assistant, I help students move from concept to physical prototype by supporting fabrication workflows, answering process questions, and helping them use equipment safely and effectively. The role has given me direct exposure to how design decisions affect manufacturability, assembly, and the pace of iteration in a shared prototyping environment.

Responsibilities

  • Support students during prototyping and fabrication across machining, additive manufacturing, and laser-based processes
  • Guide safe setup and operation of lab equipment during student project work
  • Help troubleshoot fabrication issues related to geometry, tooling, fixturing, print setup, and assembly
  • Assist students in choosing appropriate manufacturing methods for different design constraints
  • Maintain an organized and safe shop environment while supporting day-to-day lab operations

How the Role Strengthened My Engineering Perspective

Manufacturing Awareness

Working in EPIC has strengthened my understanding of design for manufacturing by exposing me to how real fabrication constraints affect part geometry, tolerances, setup decisions, and iteration speed. Supporting many different projects has made me more thoughtful about choosing processes that are both practical and efficient.

Technical Communication

The role has also improved my ability to explain technical concepts clearly in a hands-on setting. Helping students who are new to machining or prototyping has reinforced the importance of clear communication, patience, and process-based problem solving.
