Overview

Boston University’s Engineering Product Innovation Center (EPIC) is a hands-on prototyping and fabrication environment that supports student design, manufacturing, and product development. EPIC includes manual and CNC machining, sheet metal tools, laser cutters, waterjets, 3D printing, metrology, welding, and other fabrication resources that support rapid design iteration. [oai_citation:1‡Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/epic/equipment/)

As a lab assistant, I help students translate ideas into physical prototypes by supporting equipment use, troubleshooting fabrication issues, and guiding process selection across a range of manufacturing workflows. The role has strengthened my understanding of how design choices affect manufacturability, setup, iteration speed, and assembly.

Responsibilities

  • Support students during prototyping and fabrication across machining, additive manufacturing, and cutting processes
  • Guide safe setup and operation of fabrication equipment during student project work
  • Troubleshoot issues related to geometry, tooling, print setup, fixturing, and assembly
  • Help students choose appropriate manufacturing processes for their design constraints
  • Maintain a safe, organized, and efficient lab environment while supporting daily operations

How the Role Strengthened My Engineering Perspective

Manufacturing Awareness

Working in EPIC has strengthened my understanding of design for manufacturing by exposing me to how real fabrication constraints affect part geometry, tolerances, setup decisions, and iteration speed. Supporting many different projects has made me more thoughtful about choosing processes that are both practical and efficient.

Technical Communication

Helping students who are new to machining or prototyping has reinforced the importance of clear communication, patience, and process-based problem solving. It has also improved my ability to explain manufacturing decisions in a hands-on engineering environment.
