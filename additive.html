Flying Sea Turtle — FFF Print & Assembly

Multi-part articulated print produced on an Ender-series FFF printer, focusing on print setup, tolerancing, and post-print assembly rather than original part design.

Design credit: amaochan.work — original STL design. My contribution focused on fabrication, tuning, and successful operation.

Technical notes

What I tuned on the Ender + what changed print-to-print.

What I focused on

  • Print orientation and layer direction for joint strength
  • Support strategy and surface finish tradeoffs
  • Post-print cleanup and assembly sequencing
  • Tolerance behavior of moving printed joints

Outcome

Successfully printed and assembled the mechanism with smooth wing motion, demonstrating reliable articulation on a consumer-grade FFF printer without redesigning the STL.

2 kN Structural Print — Mass vs Performance

Designed and printed a structure to withstand a 2 kN compressive load while remaining above 50 mm tall during the test. [oai_citation:2‡ME 557. Project 3 - Instructions.pdf](sediment://file_000000003be871f8bcd3b3d03c2d89c4)

Material: PETG (FFF). Result: Withstood 2000 N.

What I focused on

  • Meeting the required contact geometry: 25 mm top circle and 25–50 mm bottom annulus
  • Stiffness vs mass tradeoff (shape efficiency + PETG print parameters)
  • Print orientation / perimeters / infill strategy for compressive stability
  • Avoiding buckling and local crushing under platen contact

Outcome

Produced a PETG specimen that passed the 2 kN compression requirement, maintaining height above the 50 mm threshold during loading. [oai_citation:3‡ME 557. Project 3 - Instructions.pdf](sediment://file_000000003be871f8bcd3b3d03c2d89c4)
