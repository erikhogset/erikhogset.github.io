Flying Sea Turtle — FFF Print & Assembly

Multi-part print produced on a Creality Ender-3 FFF printer, focusing on print setup, tolerancing, and post-print assembly rather than original part design.

Design credit: amaochan.work — original STL design. My contribution focused on fabrication, printer tuning, and successful operation.

Motion demo (assembled articulation).

Technical notes

What I tuned on the Ender + what changed print-to-print.

What I focused on

  • Print orientation and layer direction for joint strength
  • Support strategy and surface finish tradeoffs
  • Post-print cleanup and assembly sequencing

Successfully printed and assembled the mechanism with smooth wing motion, demonstrating reliable articulation on a consumer-grade FFF printer without redesigning the STL.

Klein Bottle — SLA Design & Print

SLA-printed Klein bottle designed to fit within the ME557 size constraints and highlight an internal geometry not possible with subtractive manufacturing. Printed in Formlabs Draft Grey Resin.

Technical notes

Design intent, print strategy, and post-processing decisions.

What I focused on

  • Geometry planning so the internal feature is visible (backlighting)
  • Support strategy and surface finish (minimize scarring on key surfaces)
  • Post-processing: wash/cure and cleanup without damaging thin sections

Outcome

Successful SLA print with the internal channel clearly visible using backlighting, plus a solid exterior finish after cleanup and post-cure.

Mass–Performance Structure — Fusion Generative Design, FFF Print & Compression Test

Compression structure developed by defining a full allowable design space between constrained faces, then using Autodesk Fusion 360 Generative Design to remove material while preserving load paths. The resulting geometry was manufactured via FFF in PETG and validated under compressive loading, successfully withstanding 2000 N.

Final PETG print produced from the Fusion Generative Design result.

What I focused on

  • Defining an appropriate design space spanning the constrained faces
  • Applying compression loads and boundary conditions in Fusion Generative
  • Orienting print layers to support compressive load paths

Outcome

The generative design process produced a lightweight structure that preserved primary load paths while eliminating nonessential material. The final PETG print met the course compression requirement and successfully sustained a 2 kN load without structural failure.

Anemometer + Weather Vane — FFF Design & Print

Desktop anemometer + vane assembly printed in Bambu PLA. The design focuses on stable rotation and reliable bearing integration for low-friction motion.

Technical notes

Key design decisions around rotation quality, stability, and print strategy.

What I focused on

  • Bearing seat geometry for a repeatable press-fit
  • Shaft alignment and stack-up tolerances across rotors
  • Balancing rotating elements to reduce wobble
  • Print orientation choices to keep bearing bores clean

Outcome

The assembly spins smoothly with a stable base. The press-fit bearing approach produced a clean, low-friction interface without adhesives, and the stacked rotor layout supported consistent rotation.
