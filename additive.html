FFF Assembly Print Tuning ME557

Flying Sea Turtle — FFF Print & Assembly

Multi-part articulated print produced on an Ender-series FFF printer, focusing on print setup, tolerancing, and post-print assembly rather than original part design.

Design credit: amaochan.work — original STL design. My contribution focused on fabrication, tuning, and successful operation.

What I focused on

  • Print orientation and layer direction for joint strength
  • Support strategy and surface finish tradeoffs
  • Post-print cleanup and assembly sequencing
  • Tolerance behavior of moving printed joints

Outcome

Successfully printed and assembled the mechanism with smooth wing motion, demonstrating reliable articulation using consumer-grade FFF equipment without redesigning the original geometry.
